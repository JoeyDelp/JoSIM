    Colored, flux and fractional noise — 6 November 2026

    This study repairs F17, an amplitude-normalization defect in the merged power-law source bank. It separately tests stochastic source units, timing and fractional device injection. It does not substitute for the #107/#108 physical SQUID/noise example, which remains separate work.

    Reproduction and model

    For the nominal 1–256 Hz band, eight seeded runs with 65536 samples each give these Welch PSD ratios at 16 Hz relative to the stated \(A_1^2/f^\alpha\):

    Exponent Before repair
    0 0.630047
    0.5 1.177373
    1 1.929505
    1.5 3.523535
    2 7.916022

    The cause is deterministic. A finite bank omits the fast/slow relaxation tails, then normalizes its continuous spectrum at 1 Hz or the nearest band edge. Moving that anchor onto a rolloff changes the interior amplitude. The endpoint exponents also have logarithmic shape errors if both omitted tails are discarded. Exact AR(1) sampling adds a further gain to fast poles. These effects exceed sampling uncertainty and do not represent the advertised interior power law.

    The repair retains the existing positive OU bank and exact AR(1) evolution. For \(0<\alpha<2\), the governing identity is

    \[ \frac{A_1^2}{f^\alpha}=4C\int_0^\infty \frac{\tau^\alpha}{1+(2\pi f\tau)^2}\,d\log\tau, \qquad C=\frac{A_1^2(2\pi)^\alpha\sin(\pi\alpha/2)}{2\pi}. \]

    Frequency is expressed numerically in Hz. The finite log-midpoint quadrature uses base variances \(C\tau_k^{\alpha-1}\delta\log\tau\). For boundary times \(\tau_f=1/(2\pi f_H)\), \(\tau_s=1/(2\pi f_L)\), the missing asymptotic tails are folded into the first/last pole as

    \[ v_{fast}=\frac{C\tau_f^\alpha}{\alpha\tau_0},\qquad v_{slow}=\frac{C\tau_s^{\alpha-2}\tau_{N-1}}{2-\alpha}. \]

    The endpoint limits are a fast-pole variance \(A_1^2/(4\tau_0)\) at exponent 0 and a slow-pole variance \(A_1^2\pi^2\tau_{N-1}\) at exponent 2. All variances are multiplied by \(\tanh q/q\), \(q=\Delta/(2\tau_k)\), to remove the exact AR(1) DC-gain increase before interpolation. expm1 preserves small innovation variance; log-domain weights avoid spurious intermediate overflow. PNOISE, FNOISE and ICNOISE use one initializer, preserving their unit relationship.

    This remains a finite-band approximation with smooth low/high turnover and interpolation rolloff. It is not an ideal sharp-cutoff power law. The OU superposition basis is described by Milotti; the weights, endpoint approximation and sampling correction above are the explicit model used here.

    Independent acceptance checks

    Runtime PSD expectations use the ideal \(A_1^2/f^\alpha\), not values obtained from JoSIM's internal bank. Eight independent seeds, 32768 samples per seed, 2048-point periodic Hann windows and half overlap give 31 segments per seed. The effective count is about 235, or 6.5% relative standard error. The predeclared 40% bound exceeds five standard errors plus the small interior approximation error. Three frequencies, 8/16/24 Hz, and five exponents give measured PSD/ideal ratios 0.865612–1.082230 after repair. This conservative short native regression is supported by the deterministic spectrum calculation below; it is not a claim of 40% model accuracy.

    At exponent 0 or 2, a single endpoint pole remains. Its closed variance and covariance provide independent expectations. For \(K\) uniformly spaced samples per update interval, covariance \(a=e^{-\Delta/\tau}\), and grid variance \(v\),

    \[ E[\widehat{\operatorname{var}}]\simeq v\left[1-(1-a)\frac{K^2-1}{3K^2}\right]. \]

    The small finite-record mean-estimation bias is inside the statistical allowance. Eight seeds cover 256 s, with longest \(\tau<0.16\) s. The conservative variance standard-error bound \(\sqrt{4\tau/T}<5\%\) justifies the predeclared 30% limit. Actual errors are below 0.9%:

    Exponent Samples/interval Measured variance Analytic variance
    0 1 231.626873 230.901854
    0 4 163.130932 162.464466
    2 1 1.46973315 1.48261342
    2 4 1.46373470 1.47662857

    Additional invariants establish:

    • Band scaling by \(c=10^{-9}\) or \(10^6\), including crossings of 1 Hz, gives \(x_c(t/c)=c^{(1-\alpha)/2}x(t)\) for all five exponents. The bound is 1e-8 times \(1+|x|\), covering floating update-clock/logarithm roundoff.
    • Dense versus skipped-tick evaluation gives exactly the same samples at common times for N=4/48/512, including before/after delay. The timestep floor matches an explicit effective interval exactly; zero ASD gives zero output.
    • FNOISE gives \(2\pi(\Phi_{DC}+x)\) radians for the same controlled realization at all five exponents, within 1e-11 rad. Standalone ICNOISE is exactly the dimensionless PNOISE sequence. The existing inductor check establishes flux linkage, and phase-source tests cover applied samples in both analysis modes.
    • Actual gated junction current in both modes equals \(I_c g\max(0.01,1+\delta)\sin\phi\), within 1e-10 A. Small fluctuations and a deliberately large case exercise the lower clipping bound. The PSD tests apply to the small-signal source; clipped noise no longer has a Gaussian PSD.
    • Same-process odd/even reseeding and rewind, independent runs, and full-cycle timestep retry remain required. Invalid timing/process counts fail cleanly.

    Approximation and compatibility limits

    A separate deterministic calculation evaluates the specified finite bank's exact sampled/interpolated spectrum. It audits approximation error; it is not the independent runtime oracle. The scan covers nine exponents from 0 to 2, band ratios 100/256/1e4/1e8, 121 logarithmic frequency positions between \(10f_L\) and \(f_H/10\), and N=4/48/512. The default N=48 gives ideal-PSD ratios 0.975331–1.000137, while N=512 gives 0.978863–1.0000005. Remaining upper-band interpolation loss does not disappear by increasing N. N=4 reaches 0.495881–1.527313: accepting the minimum process count does not promise accurate spectra across eight decades. Use more processes and check the measurement's band and timestep. Near turnover edges, larger deviations are expected; sufficiently narrow bands have no well-separated interior.

    Source syntax, class/struct layouts, units and existing signatures are retained. A private initializer removes duplicated bank construction. Corrected weights and endpoint ordering change seeded colored waveforms. A1 now consistently specifies the ideal interior law's extrapolated 1 Hz amplitude; it does not promise the actual rolloff PSD at 1 Hz. Public debug field scale is 1 because stationary variances include amplitude; S1Hz is a continuous-OU diagnostic, before sampling/interpolation. See the noise guide.
