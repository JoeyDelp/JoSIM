    Floating references, flux and SQUID noise — 6 November 2026

    Two bounded examples address the support questions in #107/#108. The finite-L example checks electrical references and external mutual flux. A separate zero-L reduction provides an independently checkable classical noise response. Neither implements quantum dynamics, nonequilibrium quasiparticle noise or a general magnetic-field solver. The user guide gives commands.

    Floating primary and finite-L equilibrium

    The primary has L=100 pH and a 1-ohm series resistor. Its voltage source is between two non-ground nodes. Mutuals to the two 5 pH SQUID arms are -5/+5 pH. Their squared coupling coefficients sum to 0.1, below 1, so the three-inductor energy matrix is positive definite. With no transport bias, KCL gives opposite arm currents. Flux linkage and the sinusoidal current-phase relation give

    \[ 2\sigma\arcsin(I/I_c)+(L_1+L_2)I=(M_2-M_1)I_P,\qquad \sigma=\frac{h_{Planck}}{4\pi e}. \]

    For \(I_c=1\) mA and applied flux \(0.25\Phi_0\), independent scalar bisection gives \(I_P=51.6958462115\) uA, circulating current 48.5021253127 uA and junction phases \(\pm0.0485211619925\) rad. The 24 native cases cover two modes, 1/0.5 ps steps, both flux polarities and 1-ohm/1-megohm/1-gigaohm reference resistors. After 10 ns, assertions require current error <=20 pA, phase error <=1e-8 rad, flux residual <5e-7 Phi0 and reference current/KCL residual <1 fA. The budgets include CSV and retained physical-constant rounding. Measured maximum flux residual is 3.713e-9 Phi0. Removing the reference gives a useful singular-matrix error in both modes, after removing the now-invalid reference current trace request too.

    The anchor fixes the island's common-mode voltage and carries no loop current in this circuit. It has TEMP=0 so a global thermal setting cannot turn the numerical anchor into an unintended noise source. Other circuits can have physical common-mode paths; the anchor's insignificance must be checked there. This establishes a floating voltage source and applied loop flux, independently of claims about a complete SQUID amplifier or its parasitics.

    Independent weak-noise reference

    For equal junctions with negligible loop inductance and external flux f in flux quanta, the two sinusoidal currents reduce to one effective RSJ:

    \[ I_c^{eff}=2I_c\cos(\pi f),\quad R_{eff}=R_N/2,\quad C_{eff}=2C, \qquad \dot\psi=\frac{R_{eff}}{\sigma}(I_b-I_c^{eff}\sin\psi+\delta I). \]

    The circuit retains finite C; the reference neglects its effect in the strongly overdamped, weak-noise limit. At f=0.25, each Ic=1 mA/RN=1 milliohm/C=10 pF, and Ib=1.6 mA, the independent SI values are:

    Quantity Value
    Effective Ic / R / C 1.41421356 mA / 0.5 milliohm / 20 pF
    Mean voltage, R sqrt(Ib²-Ic_eff²) 0.374165739 uV
    Josephson frequency, mean voltage/Phi0 180.945746 MHz
    Effective plasma frequency 73.772542 GHz
    beta_c / Gamma=kBT/(Ic_eff sigma) 2.14857e-5 / 1.24590e-4
    Classical scale kBT/h at 4.2 K 87.513800 GHz

    Let \(D=I_b^2-(I_c^{eff})^2\). Transform the noiseless running phase to a uniform angle theta. Its small-noise phase-response factor is \(Z(\theta)=(I_b+I_c^{eff}\cos\theta)/\sqrt D\). The constant and first harmonic mix current noise into low-frequency voltage noise, giving

    \[ S_V(f)\simeq\frac{R_{eff}^2}{D}\left[ I_b^2S_I(f)+\frac{(I_c^{eff})^2}{4} \{S_I(f_J+f)+S_I(f_J-f)\}\right]. \]

    The two independent shunts supply \(S_I(f)=4k_BT/R_{eff}\;\operatorname{sinc}^4(f/(3NEB))\). This reference uses exact SI constants and the independently derived interpolation transfer, not JoSIM output or fitted amplitudes. In the broad-white limit at f≈0 it reduces to \(4k_BTR_{eff}[I_b^2+(I_c^{eff})^2/2]/D\); for noise far narrower than fJ, the mixing term is suppressed. This is why bandwidth can affect measured low-frequency noise even in an overdamped circuit.

    Protocol and measured acceptance

    tools/qualify_squid_noise.py owns the fixed scientific campaign; optional dependencies are pinned in tools/requirements-qualification.txt. Sixteen seeds at each of three bandwidths, plus four noiseless mode/step cases and nine paired refinements, give 61 runs. Each discards 100 ns and measures 5 us. The base internal step is 2 ps, output spacing 20 ps, with no FIR. Verbose output verifies the final internal step and computed count, so a full-run retry cannot silently change the experiment.

    Welch analysis uses 0.5 us periodic-Hann windows, 50% overlap and separate 4/8/12/16/20 MHz bins. Each seed's primary statistic is the mean of its five PSD/reference ratios. The between-seed standard error gives a Student-t interval, using a Bonferroni correction for nominal 99% family confidence over three bands (critical t=3.483677, 15 degrees of freedom). A predeclared 5% systematic allowance covers weak-noise/finite-C/low-frequency and window approximations; acceptance is |ensemble mean ratio-1| <= statistical half-width +0.05. This is an aggregate-band criterion; individual bins retain sampling scatter. No amplitude is fitted to the simulator.

    NEB NEB/fJ Mean PSD/reference Statistical half-width
    20 MHz 0.110530 1.020164 0.067364
    200 MHz 1.105304 0.998104 0.123052
    2 GHz 11.053037 0.992709 0.064432

    All pass the declared criterion. Actual mean measured voltage PSDs across the five bins are 0.401566/0.638990/0.730640 pV²/Hz, compared with independent 0.393702/0.640216/0.736007. At the lowest bandwidth, the measurement band also approaches the noise rolloff; the reference explicitly includes that attenuation. The higher-band changes include noise mixed down from near fJ. Every tested bandwidth remains far below the bare plasma frequency; this particular strongly overdamped model does not require driving noise through that resonance.

    Paired seed-101 checks hold the physical noise interval fixed. Halving h to 1 ps changes any primary-bin PSD by at most 0.1413%. Halving output spacing to 10 ps changes it by at most 0.0612%. Switching analysis mode changes it by at most 9.059e-7 relative. Each is below the predeclared 1% refinement bound. Noiseless periods err by 1.9803 ppm at 2 ps and 0.5138 ppm at 1 ps in both modes, improving about fourfold. Mean-voltage error is below 0.164 nV, inside the 1 nV finite-record allowance. Noiseless period allowance is 10 ppm.

    The sampled PSD equation assumes weak classical fluctuations, fixed bias, negligible screening and low measurement frequencies relative to fJ. It does not justify a universal NEB>fJ or NEB>fP prescription. Underdamped resonances, larger noise, switching, finite loop inductance or other noise mechanisms need their own physical model and bandwidth/timestep convergence checks.

    Defects uncovered by the example

    F18 rejects excess NOISE/PNOISE/FNOISE/ICNOISE arguments, previously discarded silently in four reproduced cases. Direct-library and clean-CLI tests protect the documented syntax. Verbose output also now reports effective timestep and computed count; a 2 ps requested grid reduced to 0.25 ps reports 512 actual points over 128 ps in both modes.

    F19 repairs output selection. A 1 ps internal grid with 10 ps print spacing previously emitted 9/10/11 ps gaps because cumulative target addition crossed floating grid ties inconsistently. Select targets directly with fma and a few-ulp tie allowance; do not interpolate output. Both modes and integer/ fractional starts check all 1990 timestamps against independent integer-index expectations. General nonintegral step ratios still select actual nonuniform internal samples. Device equations, physical constants and file columns are unchanged; corrected output sample choices intentionally change affected files.

    Source/fixture/binary hashes, exact decks, logs, per-seed PSDs, uncertainty and figures are under artifacts/audit-resolution/squid-noise/. The exact final 61-run owner is preserved there as qualification-owner.py, matching the current tool's recorded hash. It verifies reported seeds and effective steps, and checks source/binary stability. Both PNG and PDF were generated; the plot was inspected. Raw reproducible transient CSVs were removed after analysis; their hashes and complete generation inputs remain. Peak child RSS was 145.7 MiB; concurrent build/sanitizer activity means these timings are not a controlled performance benchmark. Native numerical receipts and red print-grid witnesses are under build/audit-resolution/squid-example/.

    The candidate is based on cb594a3 plus the F18/F19/example changes. All four GCC16/Clang23 Debug/Release configurations pass 33/33; the full ASan/UBSan suite with leak detection passes 33/33 in 209.52 s. A subsequent explicit standard-header include was compiled in all four CLI builds; numerical behavior was unchanged. Native results for the implementing revision remain pending in the audit ledger. Final integration/release/site qualification remains separate.
