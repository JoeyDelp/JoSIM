    Model-IV state and timing qualification — 6 November 2026

    This study resolves F23 and extends the gate/model-IV review. The separate retry study repairs bias loss during timestep reduction. Here the two concerns are initial-state contamination and an unconfigurable finite averaging protocol. The result remains a transient model testbench, with explicit preparation and measurement settings.

    Initial state

    For an ordinary sinusoidal RSJ junction, the constitutive equations are odd under \((I,\phi,V)\mapsto(-I,-\phi,-V)\). Starting from the same zero state with sign-reversed bias ramps must therefore give sign-reversed voltage traces, including before a DC average has converged.

    The original model-IV owner reused one Matrix across distinct Simulation objects. Simulation reset its solution vector, but per-device histories remained from the preceding point. For ICrit=100µA, RN=1mΩ, CAP=0, RTYPE=0 and paired ±150µA excursions, the default protocol produced +135.1658 nV and −138.4165 nV: an odd-symmetry residual of −3.2507 nV. Returning zero-current branches also had a 1.385687 nV symmetry residual.

    Changing only matrix ownership—rebuilding it from the point's declared ramp— gave ±135.1658 nV and opposite ∓1.023181 nV return means. Both symmetry residuals are zero at CSV precision. Each point now starts with fresh device histories. Its Input declaration also remains synchronized for retries. This isolates the cause from steady-state averaging accuracy; no junction equation or physical constant changed.

    Timing controls and support boundary

    .IV now accepts TSTEP, TSTOP and TSTART, in seconds, for the initial internal step, run duration and averaging start. Defaults retain 0.05 ps, 500 ps and half the selected duration. The 0/10/50 ps preparation knots remain: outward points ramp from zero to the point, while returning points first visit the corresponding ±Imax. Points do not carry the preceding final state.

    Require finite TSTEP > 0, TSTOP > TSTART >= 50 ps, a supported requested grid and at least two actual averaging samples. The mean uses unfiltered internal samples at/after TSTART; the stop time itself is not an extra sample. The phase guard may reduce the step, which never increases within the curve. Verbose level 1 reports requested timing and each point's actual step/count.

    Existing public signatures and class layouts remain. Direct do_simulate callers with default prstart=0 retain the latter-half convention; a positive prstart now selects the averaging start. One-sample averages fail explicitly. Fresh state changes the incorrect historical finite-window results. The CSV format, current order/endpoints and ramp definitions remain.

    The finite protocol has no universal DC-accuracy guarantee. A longer duration can reduce settling and finite-cycle error; a smaller step checks discretization. These controls do not add Newton iteration, adaptive error tolerances or continuous hysteresis. Use an explicit circuit transient for a different preparation history. The sweep guide gives the command and limits.

    Independent slow-RSJ/gate reference

    For C=0, constant resistance, sinusoidal CPR and |I|>|Ic,eff|, the RSJ mean is

    \[ \overline V=\operatorname{sgn}(I)R_N\sqrt{I^2-I_{c,\mathrm{eff}}^2},\qquad I_{c,\mathrm{eff}}=I_c\max(0,1-|V_g|/GWIDTH). \]

    With I=150µA, Ic=100µA and RN=1mΩ, the ungated reference is 111.8033989 nV, with Josephson period about 18.5 ns. The short 500 ps default gives 135.1658 nV, 20.90% above the DC mean even after state repair. A user previously had no model-IV timing controls with which to refine it.

    Use TSTEP=20p TSTOP=5u TSTART=1u: the source is on its final plateau long before averaging. The independently expected and measured positive means are:

    Gate, GWIDTH=10mV Effective Ic RSJ mean JoSIM mean Relative difference
    Ungated or 0mV 100µA 111.8034 nV 111.7475 nV −0.0500%
    +5mV or −5mV 50µA 141.4214 nV 141.4591 nV +0.0267%
    10mV 0 150 nV 150 nV 0

    Negative excursions and both zero-current return rows are also checked. The positive/negative gate curves agree at CSV precision. Ordinary and zero-gate curves agree. Both caller analysis modes are represented; model IV itself uses voltage analysis, as before.

    The running RSJ solution's periodic phase correction spans less than 2π. Its continuous finite-window voltage mean therefore differs from the long-run mean by at most Phi0/W, where W is the averaging interval. Here W≈4µs, giving about 0.517 nV. Add a predeclared 0.1 nV allowance for discrete stepping/sample averaging: the resulting bound is about 0.617 nV. This is independent of the measured mean; it does not fit the observed error.

    Halving the ungated step from 20 ps to 10 ps changes the mean by only 2e-13 V = 0.0002 nV, well below the separate 0.1 nV discretization allowance. The observed effective steps are exactly the requested values, with 200,000 or 400,000 averaging samples. An eight-run campaign includes two short-protocol checks and six long/refined/gated runs, recording every row, actual seed, timing/count, source/binary hashes and unchanged identities.

    Regression and evidence

    regression::gate has 16 methods. New checks cover short-protocol sign symmetry, six independent long/refined/gated curves with metadata, and invalid timing including empty/one-sample windows. Its existing gate-temperature, waveform, scope, node, endpoint and repeated-retry assertions remain.

    All four local GCC 16.2.1/Clang 23.1.4 Debug/Release configurations pass 34/34 CTest groups. Full Clang Debug ASan/UBSan and leak detection pass 34/34 in 237.00 s, including the expanded 16-method gate group in 31.19 s. Native qualification remains required for the pushed revision; it is not inferred from these local checks.

    ctest --test-dir build/dev -R 'regression::gate$' --output-on-failure

    Detailed evidence:

    • build/audit-resolution/iv-physics/state-{red,green}.json: one-variable state-reset experiment with exact source/binary hashes and symmetry residuals.
    • artifacts/audit-resolution/iv-physics/timing/report.json: eight-run campaign; adjacent per-case decks, CSV, logs and qualification-owner.py preserve the complete method. Run the archived owner from the source checkout's root.
    • artifacts/audit-resolution/iv-physics/: compact state/retry receipts and per-configuration build/test/sanitizer results; detailed logs remain under build/audit-resolution/iv-physics/.

    The campaign source and binary stayed unchanged during execution. Native tests need only Python's standard library; these expectations do not depend on the optional plotting/scientific packages. The measured physical reference is a deterministic overdamped RSJ limit, not a proof of convergence for arbitrary capacitive, gap-transition, noisy or nonequilibrium models.
