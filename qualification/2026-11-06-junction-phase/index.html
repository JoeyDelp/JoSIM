    Junction phase accumulation — 6 November 2026

    Issue #91 reports long-run junction artifacts without a reproducing circuit. This study identifies a concrete defect capable of producing that symptom. It does not infer the reporter's exact circuit or explain every possible artifact.

    Reproduction and cause

    Drive an RCSJ junction with an ideal 1 mV source, IC=100u, RN=10, C=0.1p, RTYPE=0, a cold start, and a 0.01 ps timestep. Inspect raw double-precision results, rather than rounded CSV phase values. Once the startup transient has passed, Josephson's relation requires

    \[ \phi(t)-\phi(t_a)=\frac{V}{\sigma}(t-t_a). \]

    Anchoring at sample 100 removes the arbitrary phase origin and the cold-start transient. The old voltage-mode matrix uses SIGMA_H = HBAR/(2*EV) = 3.2910597572337034e-16 V*s, while its history RHS uses SIGMA = 3.291059757e-16 V*s. Their relative difference is only rho - 1 = -7.10116e-11, but the resulting recurrence is

    \[ \phi_n=\rho\left(\frac43\phi_{n-1}-\frac13\phi_{n-2}\right) +\frac{2hV}{3\sigma_H}. \]

    Its nominal unit root acquires a false decay of approximately 1 - 1.06517e-10 per step. A 70-digit independent evaluation of this recurrence predicts the measured drift within 2.4 ppm across all four run lengths:

    Steps Nominal duration Old voltage-mode phase error
    10,000 0.1 ns 0.000161796 rad
    100,000 1 ns 0.0161826 rad
    1,000,000 10 ns 1.61822 rad
    10,000,000 100 ns 161.771 rad

    Phase mode does not show this false decay. Its worst error against the legacy SIGMA reference in the 10-million-step probe is 8.85e-5 rad. The difference between the legacy physical constant and exact SI definitions contributes about 0.00256 rad over 100 ns; it cannot explain the voltage-mode defect. The exact SI reference is sigma = h/(4*pi*e) using the NIST flux-quantum relation.

    Repair and acceptance

    Derive both JJ matrix coefficients from the existing Constants::SIGMA, the same scale already used by history terms, sources and output conversion. All physical constant values remain unchanged. This removes the inconsistent recurrence coefficient; it does not wrap phase or discard accumulated cycles.

    test/junction_numerics.cpp supplies 24 raw-result cases: both modes, positive and negative 1 mV bias, 0.01/0.1 ps timesteps, and 10k/100k/1m samples. It checks finite data, the independent Josephson relation, constant voltage, KCL, and I = Ic*sin(phi) + V/R after the constant-voltage capacitive transient.

    The phase allowance is 8*epsilon*abs(V*h/SIGMA)*N^2 + 1e-10 rad, with epsilon the double machine precision and N the sample count. It is a conservative accumulated-rounding allowance, rather than a fitted physical error or a relaxed comparison to the broken result. Constitutive current, KCL and voltage allowances are 1e-10 A, 1e-15 A and 1e-10 V respectively. The old code fails even the shortest case by more than four orders of magnitude relative to its phase allowance.

    GCC16/Clang23 Debug and Release pass all 32 tests. The full ASan/UBSan suite, with leak detection, passes 32/32 in 175.12 s; the raw junction group takes 28.06 s. The 24 constant-bias cases have at most 6.88e-6 rad drift, below 4.4% of their rounding allowances. Commit 69b9f1d3b6f passes all ten native binary/library jobs in CI 42233555152.

    The extended 10-million-step green probe reduces voltage-mode drift from 161.771 rad to 8.736e-5 rad, a factor of about 1.85 million. Phase-mode drift is 8.844e-5 rad. Both are now at accumulated-rounding scale. The separate legacy-versus-SI scale difference remains visible as about 0.00248 rad over this duration. No physical constants were silently updated.

    The eight extended runs complete in 1.78 s with peak RSS about 411.4 MiB on the local Linux/Clang Release build. This measures a four-unknown deterministic circuit with retained raw histories; it is not the required large/noisy-circuit performance qualification. Commands and resource output are preserved. Detailed local evidence: build/audit-resolution/long-phase-red.json, long-phase-initial.csv, junction-numerics-red.log, and artifacts/audit-resolution/junction-constant-bias.csv and junction-long-phase.json. Red evidence includes the source/library identities, probe hash, high-precision prediction and resource measurements. The final report will publish compact evidence durably.

    Current-driven switching, pulse counts, nonlinear convergence and the #101 buffer study remain separate numerical requirements. This repair establishes a cause and protects constant-bias phase accumulation; it does not establish arbitrary nonlinear-circuit accuracy.
