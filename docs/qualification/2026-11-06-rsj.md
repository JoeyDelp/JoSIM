# Current-driven RSJ qualification — 6 November 2026

This study supplements the constant-voltage phase regression with independent
nonlinear equilibrium, rotation-period and voltage-pulse-area references. It
uses the capacitance-free RSJ limit: `RTYPE=0`, `C=0`, `Ic=100u` and `RN=10`.
Temperature, spread, gate control and noise are disabled. Here the model's
constant shunt resistance is RN; this does not select a resistance convention
for the nonlinear `RTYPE=1` model.

## Independent physical expectations

\[
\dot\phi=\frac{R}{\sigma}(I_b-I_c\sin\phi).
\]

At `Ib=50u` the zero-start solution settles to `phi=asin(0.5)=pi/6` and zero
voltage, without complete rotations. At `Ib=200u`, integrating this equation
over a complete turn gives

\[
T=\frac{\sigma}{R}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\phi}{I_b-I_c\sin\phi}
 =\frac{2\pi\sigma}{R\sqrt{I_b^2-I_c^2}},\qquad
\overline V=R\sqrt{I_b^2-I_c^2}.
\]

Using JoSIM's declared legacy `SIGMA`, the period is 1.19386441898 ps and the
mean voltage is 1.73205080757 mV. This continuous reference uses no JoSIM
trajectory or discrete recurrence. Separately, the voltage area per 2*pi turn
is compared with exact SI `h/(2e)`, independently of `Constants::PHI_ZERO`.

## Measurement and results

The 12 cases cover both modes, both bias regimes, and 0.02/0.01/0.005 ps steps
over 1 ns with startup disabled. Raw phase crossings after 20 ps define complete
rotations; fractional crossing times and voltage integrals use linear endpoint
interpolation. Each rotating case measures 820–821 complete periods. Subcritical
cases have no complete rotations.

| Step | Relative period error | Error after halving |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.02 ps | 567.504 ppm | — |
| 0.01 ps | 142.585 ppm | 0.2513 of previous |
| 0.005 ps | 35.6945 ppm | 0.2503 of previous |

Both modes give these values at the shown precision. Measured period convergence
is approximately second order on these grids. Maximum mean pulse-area error is
0.092 ppm against exact SI. Maximum KCL residual is 5.43e-20 A. In the local
Clang Release measurement, the subcritical phase differs from pi/6 by less than
2e-14 rad and the final voltage magnitude is below 1.5e-17 V.

The regression allows relative period error `4*(h/T)^2`, and each refinement
must reduce it below `0.4*previous + 1e-9`. The small floor permits accumulated
raw-double rounding rather than demanding further reduction below numerical
precision. Equilibrium phase/voltage allowances are 1e-9 rad and 1e-12 V; KCL
allows 1e-15 A. Averaged pulse area must agree with exact SI within 1 ppm,
including endpoint integration and the historical constant's 0.0084 ppm scale
difference. This is an average over complete rotations, not a per-pulse shape
accuracy claim.

## Reproduction and limits

```bash
ctest --test-dir build/dev -R regression::junction_numerics --output-on-failure --no-tests=error
build/dev/test/josim-junction-regression rsj
```

The default CTest selection also executes the 24 constant-voltage cases. The
`constant`/`rsj` arguments select separate tabular measurements. GCC16/Clang23 Debug and Release pass all 36 junction cases. ASan/UBSan with
leak detection passes the same cases in 33.58 s. Native-platform qualification
passes all ten native binary/library jobs for `04ef05788e7a` in
[CI 42235902594](https://github.com/JoeyDelp/JoSIM/actions/runs/42235902594).
Machine-readable measurements are under `artifacts/audit-resolution/rsj.csv`
and `rsj.json`; detailed build/test logs are under `build/audit-resolution/`.
The final qualification will publish compact evidence durably.

The RSJ limit establishes these specific physical relationships. It does not
qualify finite-capacitance switching transients, a clocked SFQ circuit, stochastic
escape, gap-region transitions or the supplied #101 buffer. Those remain separate
requirements. No production equations changed in this test addition.
