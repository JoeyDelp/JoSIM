# Finite sine cycles (#108) — 6 November 2026

The original requested source `SINE(0 315u 4g 20p 0 0 4)` is implemented.
Its stop time is

\[
t_{end}=T_D+N_C/f=20\text{ ps}+4/(4\text{ GHz})=1020\text{ ps}.
\]

A 1-ohm load provides independent current/voltage checks in both analysis
modes, at 5 ps steps through 1100 ps. Applied source current, resistor current
and voltage must match the analytic sine within 1 nA/1 nV, including zero
before the delay and after the fourth cycle. The assertions require samples
in the active peaks and after the stop, rather than merely a successful run.

Direct waveform tests cover SIN/SINE aliases, nonzero offset, 30-degree phase,
damping, delayed start, default frequency, fractional cycles and zero/negative
cycle counts. The expected equation is calculated independently using standard
sin/exp, including both sides of the cutoff. A positive cycle count specifies
duration N_C/f after the delay; phase does not change that duration. Nonpositive
or omitted N_C selects an unlimited tone. Stopping returns to the DC offset,
which can be discontinuous when the tone has a nonzero phase at its cutoff.

Validation exposed a separate defect: finite cycles with zero/negative frequency
were accepted at verbosity 0 and rejected at verbosity 1. They are now rejected
consistently. More than seven arguments also fails explicitly. Error help now
shows the phase and cycle-count fields. Valid waveform evaluation is unchanged.
