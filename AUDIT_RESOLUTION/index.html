    I088 — Nonlinear inductor #88

    • Classification: optional substantial solver feature; outside current support.
    • Disposition: JoSIM supports constant Phi=LI. Define whether the requested L(I)=L0(1+a*I^2) means differential inductance or Phi/I: their differential laws differ by a factor of three in the nonlinear term. No per-step L(I) substitution is advertised as a nonlinear solver. Full support requires a flux law/derivative, consistent initial bias/mutual semantics and convergence qualification in both modes.
    • Alternative/limits: A constant-L approximation can be used only within an assessed excursion. For the differential interpretation, abs(a*I^2)<=0.01 bounds departure from L0 to 1% at the element level; this does not bound long-line phase/gain error. Constant-L RL/RLC dynamics already have independent analytic/refinement tests. Large-signal nonlinear/JTWPA behaviour remains outside the supported model. See the derivation.
